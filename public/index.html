What every button does

Attack MoveSelect troops and press Attack Move, then click a destination. A red circle marks the area. Troops advance toward enemies inside it and attack enemy units and buildings within the circle; enemy units are prioritized before buildings. Clear groupDeselects your currently selected troops. − Zoom / + ZoomZoom the camera out or in. Move CameraEnables camera panning and rotation controls. Select ModeEnables troop selection and ground-click movement orders. Drag a box to select multiple troops. RotateToggles camera rotation while in Move Camera mode. SettingsOpens this guide and display options. LineImmediately arranges selected troops in a line; future Move orders keep this formation. ColumnImmediately arranges selected troops in a column; future Move orders keep this formation. WedgeImmediately arranges selected troops in a wedge; future Move orders keep this formation. BlockImmediately arranges selected troops in a compact block; future Move orders keep this formation. Select AllSelects all your land combat troops. RegroupOrders selected troops to gather around their current average position. Build buttonsChoose a building blueprint. Place it on valid land and confirm the placement. Recruit buttonsQueue the listed troop at the selected production building if you have enough resources and supply. Demolish buildingRemoves your selected building. The castle cannot be demolished and resources are not refunded. Realm mapClick the minimap to move the camera focus to that location. Chat SendSends your typed message to players in the realm. Confirm placementConfirms the current building placement if the location is valid. CancelCancels the current building placement.

Troops automatically attack enemy units and buildings whenever they are within their attack range.
